# Business Intent and Decision Principles

## Product goal

The Copilot reduces manager handling time while improving consistency and driver trust. It should help
a manager understand **what happened, why the driver may churn, what can be offered, and what must be
checked before action**.

## Decision principles

1. **Protect the driver relationship.** Explain the evidence and acknowledge operational friction.
2. **Do not buy silence.** Compensation is tied to documented policy conditions, not sentiment alone.
3. **Catalogue is not authority.** A returned incentive can still be non-compliant for the case.
4. **Unknown is not zero.** Missing spend and credit history block automatic execution.
5. **Prefer reversible actions.** Non-cash support and operational remediation accompany compensation.
6. **Escalate patterns, not only incidents.** Repeated airport or geofence issues should create an
   operations signal rather than repeated isolated payments.
7. **Show the manager the why.** Every action carries evidence and policy clause IDs.

## Success measures

- Reduced average manager handling time.
- Higher percentage of recommendations with complete evidence and valid citations.
- Lower policy-override and post-issuance reversal rates.
- Retention lift against a carefully controlled baseline, segmented by issue and tier.
- Stable or reduced recovery cost per retained active driver.
- Driver satisfaction after service recovery, not only short-term online status.

A production experiment must avoid selection bias: high-risk drivers should be randomised within
eligible policy bands, and retention should be measured over meaningful time windows rather than only
the next session.
