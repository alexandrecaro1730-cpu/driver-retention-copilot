## Section A: Compensation Caps & Stacking

### A.1 Global Monthly Cap
No automated retention package may exceed £150.00 in total value per driver, per calendar month.

### A.2 Credit Stacking
A driver may not receive more than two immediate credits within the same 24-hour window. If a third
issue occurs, the system must escalate to a human City Manager.

### A.3 Tier Multipliers
Goodwill credits in category Churn are strictly reserved for Gold and Silver tiers only.

## Section B: Specific Issue Resolutions

### B.1 Airport Short Fares (SFP)
Applicable only if wait time is greater than 90 minutes and trip distance is less than 3 kilometres.
Gold tier cap: £25.00 maximum per instance. Silver and Bronze tier cap: £15.00 maximum per instance.
The AI must not use INC-002 (£50) for standard short-fare recovery; it is reserved for documented
multi-day systemic failures.

### B.2 Technical & GPS Glitches
Maximum compensation is £10.00 per confirmed glitch using INC-003. Priority Support Access INC-004
should be offered for recurring geofence issues when there are more than two tickets in seven days.

### B.3 New Starters
For tenure below three months, priority should be given to Commission Shields INC-006 rather than
direct credits, to encourage continued app usage.

### B.4 Infeasible Quests
If a driver completes more than 80 percent of a Quest but fails because of documented low demand,
and diagnostics confirm fewer than 1.5 offers per hour, a Goodwill Gesture of £20.00 is permitted.
